# 0.0.38: the MRE Station, the MRE and Marines Fuel: testing

Branch `feature/drink-station`, after 0.0.37.

## How it was run

* **Unit tests** (`:eternal-defense:test`), JDK 17.
* **Real client** of each edition (Forge 47.4.20 development runtime with TaCZ, `xvfb`, 1280 x 720 at GUI scale 2), the new harness `tools/qa/QaMre.java.txt` on a fresh flat world with a Defense Beacon (the station stands in its Home Zone). It places the station with its item, fills it with real slot clicks (one item at a time, as a player right-clicks), presses the screen's real buttons, sends a cheating click packet and Make requests the screen would not allow, compares every MRE with vanilla's own `FoodData` fed each food alone (the real items, Forge's `FoodData.eat(item, stack, player)`), eats MREs in survival by holding the use key and reads the food bar at the moment the server finishes the eating, crafts Marines Fuel through the edition's recipe data, buys the station at Workshop Fabrication, opens the Field Guide, breaks the station, checks the screen's layout at three window sizes and takes screenshots.

## Results

### Unit tests: 364 of 364 pass

* New `Round0038Test` (14): over 20,000 random packs of vanilla foods (up to six, at most 20 nutrition) the MRE's nutrition is the plain sum and its saturation the exact sum of each food's own value by vanilla's formula, stored as the nearest float, at most 20; the order of the slots never changes the totals (bit for bit); the eat modifier gives the stored saturation back through vanilla's formula exactly or one float step below (19,273 exact, 727 a step below, never above); from an empty bar and from a bar with room the MRE gives what vanilla's `FoodData.eat` would, the same as the foods one by one unless vanilla's cap cut in between them (then never less); the 20 cap (14 + 6 fits, 15 + 6 does not; four bread is 20; three golden carrots store 20 saturation); the slot and the server both refuse an overflow, every check comes before anything is used up; equal totals stack to 64 and the tag holds nothing else; no effect and no always-edible in the MRE's food properties, the foods' own eating code never runs, no MRE in an MRE; containers come back from the crafting remainder or the stews' bowl; the 8th card of the Kitchen group, the price, the registrations; the Marines Fuel recipe in both editions; the owner's art installed as supplied; the Armor Platform leaves the mod's own items out; the guide's numbers are the code's.
* Changed: `LangKeysTest` (the three new cards), `Round0035Test` (the Kitchen group's end follows the list).

### Real client

* **First standalone run** (`docs/validation/round-0038/checks-standalone-first-run.txt`, 66 of 78). Real findings, fixed: the totals text "Nutrition 13 / 20" was cut short and ran over "Saturation 12.6" (now a row each: the label left, the number right, the bar under it); "Take back" was wider than its button; the screen was 248 px tall, more than a 720p window at GUI scale 3 gives (240): now 238 (no "Inventory" label above the pack). The harness's own mistakes: it placed the station in creative (the item is kept there), read the title from the text recorder (the header draws it directly), recorded a frame before the hover reached the screen, filled the "full" pack while a bowl stack still had room, and read the food bar 5 ticks after eating while Poison damage had made vanilla's regeneration spend 1 saturation (it now reads the bar in the server's eating Finish event).
* Everything else passed in that run: the station placed by its item stands 1 x 1 x 2 facing the player; real clicks refuse stone, fuel in the paper slot and paper in the fuel slot; the screen's live totals equal vanilla's exact sums; Make makes one MRE with vanilla's sums and uses one of each food, 1 fuel, 1 paper; equal MREs stack in the output and in the pack, different ones do not; a fifth food past 20 is refused on the client and the server, a cheating click packet is refused and corrected; Make sent past a disabled button is refused with nothing used up (other MREs in the output, an overflow set behind the screen's back, no paper); two bowls and a bottle come back; the MRE's tooltip; eaten in survival in 16 ticks (as long as vanilla's dried kelp), the whole amount at once, the same as the four foods one by one; not eaten with a full bar, the golden apples' always-edible ignored (a real golden apple is eaten, as control); no effect from golden apples, rotten flesh, raw chicken, pufferfish, spider eye, suspicious stew, no chorus teleport, honey does not cure Poison (a real honey bottle does, as control); nothing comes back when an MRE is eaten; Marines Fuel crafted from a clay ball and lime or black dye (2); Workshop Fabrication sells the station for 4 Ardent Energy, 8 iron and a smoker, as the Kitchen group's 8th card in the right column, bottom row; the Field Guide page; breaking the station drops it and what it holds.
* **Final runs** (after the fixes): **standalone 78 of 78** (`checks-standalone.txt`), **pack 78 of 78** (`checks-pack.txt`). The screen at 640 x 360, 480 x 270 and 427 x 240 (1280 x 720 at GUI scale 3): no text over another, a slot or a button, nothing cut short, the whole panel in the window. Eating read at the server's Finish event: P1 (bread, apple, carrot, dried kelp) 7 -> 20 food and 0 -> 12.6 saturation, the same as vanilla eating the four one by one; the four bread MRE 0 -> 20 and 20; P3 (stews, honey, spider eye) 0 -> 20 and 18.8 with Poison on and not cured; P4 (golden apples and the rest) 1 -> 20 and 20 with no effect at all. The pack edition bought the station with 4 Reinforced Beacon Plating, the standalone with 4 Ardent Energy.
* Screenshots (`docs/validation/round-0038/`, both editions): the placed station (`m01`), the empty and the ready screen (`m02`, `m03`), the screen at the three sizes (`m04`), the MRE's tooltip (`m05`), a food past 20 in red (`m06`), the Workshop's Kitchen group with the MRE Station in its 8th place (`m07`), the Field Guide page (`m08`, `m09`). In the pack run's Workshop shot the beacon's "being attacked" banner shows: a hostile mob hit the beacon (the harness turns mob spawning off only after the world has started); it has nothing to do with the MRE Station and was not looked into further.

## Not tested

* A dedicated server with two players using one station at once; modded foods (only vanilla foods were packed); the owner's pack itself; saving and loading a world with items in the station; hoppers beside it (it has no item handler, by design); the creative inventory tab; sounds were not listened to.
