## Composition and Mk IV unlocking

Vanilla ingredients give eleven ordinary bonuses. Ordinary is the default; **food groups never automatically trigger a legendary recipe**. The Mix guide is a live cookbook and server-validated effect selector. Inspect exact source icons/groups and FIELD/HOME values, then select up to three stew effects or two sandwich effects. Selecting an effect starts a manual selection; reset restores ordinary ranking. A legendary recipe reserves both groups and replaces their ordinary contributions. Selected legendary recipes cannot share groups; selected ordinary effects need contributors outside reserved groups. Unavailable additions or excessive selections are rejected; removing selected foods invalidates preparation. Selected intent is menu-local; finished meals persist their exact bonuses.

**Mk IV stew alone doubles categories.** Ordinary ×2 requires two distinct foods in the same group feeding the same effect. Legendary ×2 requires two distinct foods in EACH group (four slots). Split stacks never qualify; no third strength level. HOME remains another independent ×2. Sandwiches use one bread from a separate bread-only base slot and two or three distinct mixing fillings. Base bread is excluded from traits, groups, batch accounting and effects. Bread intentionally put in mixing slots still gives Grain/Steadiness. Bread and filling payment is atomic after output validation. Both base and output are inactive in Stew. Eight persisted hall slots: six mixes, output index 6, base bread index 7.

Any three ordinary effects can be doubled in six slots using these same-group pairs (165 combinations verified by the runtime fixture):

| Ordinary effect | Two vanilla ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| Vitality | Cooked chicken + cooked mutton |
| Fortitude | Carrot + potato |
| Steadiness | Brown mushroom + red mushroom (or bread + wheat) |
| Mobility | Cooked cod + cooked salmon |
| Might | Raw beef + cooked beef |
| Agility | Cookie + pumpkin pie |
| Fortune | Apple + glow berries |
| Recovery | Honey bottle + golden apple |
| Hearth | Rotten flesh + spider eye |
| Springy Step | Chorus fruit + melon slice |
| Swim | Raw cod + raw salmon |

Apple additionally feeds Fortune, melon additionally feeds Springy Step. Other traits remain available; use the selector to pick exactly the intended bonuses. FIELD duration is still 30 minutes. Stew tiers retain 4/7/10/12 ingredients and 4/8/12/16 servings; Mk I supports four ingredient types, Mk II–IV six.

## Tiers and lifecycle

| Tier | Pots | Servings | Ingredient units | Next upgrade pack / standalone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I | 1 | 4 | 4 | 8 Plating / 4 Energy |
| II | 2 | 8 | 7 | 16 Plating / 8 Energy |
| III | 3 | 12 | 10 | 32 Plating / 14 Energy |
| IV | 4 | 16 | 12 | Maximum |

At least one ingredient unit per distinct type, then round-robin batch deductions. Mk I cannot compose more than four distinct types in a stew batch; later tiers can fill all six. Container remainders return to the cook. Linked pots must be within eight blocks; existing hall UUID reservations prevent stealing or replacing links. Hall upgrades preserve stock, output, identity and links. New meals replace the old one. Field duration is 30 minutes / 36,000 ticks; home freezes remaining duration and doubles values without refreshing the timer. Logout/restart pauses; dimension travel preserves; death clears. Existing per-effect IDs and save encoding remain stable.

## UI and guide

The cookbook shows three recipe rows at small GUI sizes and four at larger sizes, keeping navigation clear of recipe cards. Ingredient consumption, ordinary bonuses and food group append to the **normal Forge item tooltip**. There is one tooltip, so vanilla/modded food tooltip additions can share it. Cards and pot/upgrade tooltips are separate non-item hover targets. Stew mode hides the sandwich output frame, label and sparkle and makes slot 6 inactive/unpickable on both sides; stored output returns in Sandwich mode. The 238-pixel menu fits Minecraft’s 240-pixel GUI minimum. A disabled Prepare button explains the full failure reason on hover.

All four equipment tables retain their nearby Age indicators; the current table’s frame is larger. Each table opens its own Upgrade table view, including when the gun/components are locked or no gun is equipped. A shared client tooltip component renders material icons next to names, quantities and stock for beacon, equipment, hall, platform and cannon upgrades.

The Field Guide includes current kitchen rules and a regular Legendary food mixes page with all fifteen recipes, along with v3 ordinary bonuses and v4 sunlight/adaptation/backend behavior. `en_us.json` owns guide prose; the kitchen generator no longer writes guide keys. This fixes the v3 guide regression caused by regenerating old v2 prose and the absence of v4 guide updates.

### Legendary ingredient links

The menu draws colored animated connectors between the distinct ingredient slots consumed by each selected legendary mix. Contributing slots share the legendary buff icon. Hover a connector or icon for the two food-group names, resulting buff and Mk IV doubling requirement; the normal ingredient tooltip also names its legendary pairing. A doubled Mk IV recipe connects all four contributing slots. Links use the authoritative server preview and never imply that duplicate stacks add strength.

## Weapon compatibility and recovery shovel

Audited author pack **Apocalypse_v1.1.4_F.zip**, CurseForge file 6308196. `bf1:ef46` (Einstossflammenwerfer 46) and `bf1:wex` use `reload.type: fuel`; their author refills go through Field Radio/native station recipes, incompatible with our closed native-table path. No safe hand-reload/unjam adaptation was established with the owner’s exact addon versions, so the authorized fallback is applied: **exclude all indexed FUEL guns** from Universal catalogues, Supply recipes and direct craft requests. Explicit `bf1:ef46` rejection also works if its index is absent/replaced. Both editions enforce it; missing gun indices remain invalid. Already spawned/command-given third-party guns retain their native behavior.

Author source/download: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/customization/tacz-apocalypse-gun-pack/files/6308196 . Optional Gun Durability source researched: https://github.com/Corrinedev/tacz-durability . The gunpack is All Rights Reserved and is used only in local QA; it is **not** redistributed in the test ZIP.

The recovery shovel’s base attack total is zero. Its Forge AttackEntityEvent handler cancels normal damage even when Strength/Might/other boosts exist and applies intrinsic Knockback II (1.0 native knockback strength, subject to target resistance). It preserves existing recovery/controller actions. Creative contents are ordered by campaign tools, equipment tables, kitchen, support, currency/parts, displays and factory materials/ammo. Only Mk I is listed as **Mess Hall**; earned Mk II–IV items keep their tier names.

## Milk and dispenser interaction

Milk Dispenser is 1 wide × 1 deep × 2 tall, sold in Workshop Fabrication for **8 iron + 4 glass + 4 copper** in both editions. Bowl price remains 8 iron + 4 glass + 4 planks. **Empty both main/off hands, crouch, right-click** to stock either dispenser; holding an item displays the exact instruction. Normal right-click uses one bowl or a milk dose. Offhand invocation cannot dispense a second time. Both halves forward to the anchor and enforce the beacon zone. Bowl retains one bowl-only slot (64).

Milk is Forge’s enabled `minecraft:milk` fluid in an **8,000 mB FluidTank**, with a milk-only Forge fluid capability for pipes. Its menu has one milk-container input, one output for returned empties, and a synchronized fill bar. Buckets carry 1,000 mB; Arsenal Milk Bottles carry 250 mB. Other real Forge milk-container items are supported through their fluid capability, without name guessing. Refill accepts an entire container only when its liquid AND returned empty fit; blocked/full transfers change nothing. Stored milk and pending containers persist. Breaking drops inventory once and loses tank liquid.

Normal right-click spends **250 mB**, calls `PlayerMeals.clear` to remove saved timer and owned native/TaCZ UUID modifiers, then removes every potion effect. Empty tanks do nothing. Cleanup does not erase unrelated external attribute modifiers. Milk bottles can also be drunk for the same cleanup, return glass, stack to 16, and craft four at a time from one milk bucket + four bottles (bucket returned). No new required addon.
