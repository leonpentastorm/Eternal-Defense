New `EndlessEffects` (the guard: losses counted from `MobEffectEvent.Remove`/`Expired` at home, the mod's own removals excluded with `own`, the long timer `STEADY`, its top-up `REFRESH`, the culprit from the stack, `EndlessEvents`); `PlayerDrinks.sync`/`ours`/`strip`, `PlayerMeals.syncEffects`/`zoneEffect` and `MilkDispenser.cleanse` use it. * **Decisions and interpretations to confirm with the owner.**
  1. Version 0.0.37 (the next after 0.0.36).
  2. Creative keeps the bowl and the cup (vanilla never uses up items in creative); survival uses them up. The Latte already used its cup.
  3. The flicker: in the development runtime (vanilla, TaCZ, this mod) a Haste drink at home held still for minutes, alone, with a meal, beside a vanilla beacon giving Haste, across the zone's edge: no removal at all. So another mod in the owner's pack takes the endless effect away (many read the endless time, -1, as run out) and the tracker re-grants it every half second. Not knowing the mod, the guard stops the fight: after two losses of one effect within 30 s that effect gets a 20-minute timer at home instead of endless (a timer is what such mods leave alone), topped up in place every minute; the player is told once; the server log line `[Eternal Defense] ... was taken away ... last by <class.method:line>` names the code. **Owner: please send that log line (latest.log) so the real cause can be fixed.**
  4. The guard is per player and effect and resets when the player logs out or the server restarts (then the effect is endless again until the next two losses).
  5. A totem of undying clears effects once: one loss, not two, so it does not trip the guard.
* **Validation.** Unit tests 350 pass. Real client (`tools/qa/QaDrinkLoop.java.txt`, 26 checks): standalone 26 of 26, pack 26 of 26. ## Fire support rework: Ablaze, Spotter Arrows, a curse until death (0.0.36, branch `feature/drink-station`)

* **What it is.** The owner's message after the 0.0.35 screenshots, second part: "Fire support rework (next version number after the current head)". 0.0.35 was finished, built and pushed first (`3dabb76`); this round is 0.0.36 on the same branch. Three fire supports change behavior, names and text; their type ids and places in `CannonUpgrades.FireType` stay (saved by position). Rules: GDD 0.0.36. Tests: `docs/ROUND-0036-TESTING.md`.
* **Code map.** New `FireMarks` (the two marks, pure numbers and `MarkEvents`: an early-exit `LivingTickEvent` for Ablaze, a `LivingAttackEvent` that drops vanilla's own burn on an Ablaze mob, the `LivingHurtEvent` multiplier for Marked, the `LivingKnockBackEvent` cancel for a Spotter hit, clearing on death), registered in `ArsenalBeacon`. `SupportFlares`: `napalmTick` calls `FireMarks.ignite`; the `ARROW` case hits and marks every mob in the box and drops arrows that hit nothing and vanish (`SHOW_ARROW_TICKS`); the `CURSE` case puts `CannonUpgrades.CURSES` on endless, from the flare; `arrowPotion` is gone. `CannonUpgrades`: `spotterHit`, `CURSES`, `Facts.ablaze` (the menu's ninth argument). Lang: `support.name.fire_arrow`, `cannon.type.arrow*`, `cannon.type.curse.effect/.damage`, `cannon.type.napalm.effect/.damage`, `cannon.up.damage.desc`, the guide's `support.body` and `support.detail`. Tests: `Round0036Test`; `RaidFixesTest` (nine menu arguments); `HandTunedRoundTest` (the tipped-arrow test removed). QA: `tools/qa/QaFire.java.txt`.
* **Decisions and interpretations to confirm with the owner.**
  1. Version 0.0.36: "the next version number after the current head" (0.0.35).
  2. Ablaze damage is 4 a second times the Damage upgrade of the napalm's owner when it set the mob Ablaze; set Ablaze again by a stronger napalm, it keeps the stronger burn (never the sum). A raid boss takes half.
  3. "By our own timer": once a second per mob (spread by entity id), damage type `on_fire`, through `RaidAdaptation.weaponDamage`; vanilla's own 1 a second burn is cancelled while a mob is Ablaze, so it takes exactly 4 a second, not 5. Each burn comes on top of everything else: it ignores vanilla's half second of invulnerability after a hit and leaves that window as it was. Without that, a burn landing just before the carpet's hit cut that hit from 5 to 1 (seen in the second client run), and a burn just before a sword or arrow hit would have taken 4 off it. A mob that gets Fire Resistance after it was set Ablaze (a witch drinks one when burning) takes nothing while the potion lasts, as vanilla fire; the mark stays.
  4. "Re-ignite visually when not in water": out of water and rain the vanilla flames are lit again whenever they get low. In rain vanilla puts out a moving mob every tick with a hiss, so relighting there would hiss twenty times a second: in rain the mark shows as flame particles instead (the damage goes on). In water nothing shows.
  5. The napalm carpet itself is unchanged, as asked: a raider with AI walking on its fire blocks still takes vanilla's contact fire, which a raid counts as a fire trap (pre-existing; the carpet's own 5 a second and Ablaze are weapon damage). Say if the carpet's fire blocks should count as the weapon too.
  6. Spotter Arrows' hit is an `arrow`-type damage from the flare, owned by the thrower (kill credit and loot for the thrower, as the shells). Its 4 is scaled by the Damage upgrade; the menu's per-hit figure shows it. Endermen dodge it (vanilla: they teleport away from projectiles) but are marked all the same.
  7. Marked multiplies every hit by 1.30 (a raid boss 1.15) in `LivingHurtEvent`, before armor; damage tagged `bypasses_invulnerability` (`/kill`, the void) is left alone. Glowing is an endless effect, so it survives the Starshell's and Reconnaissance's shorter glowing.
  8. The falling arrows: three over each of the first ten mobs, the rest at random (as before); they cannot hit anything and vanish 2 s after they land, so nothing is left lying around.
  9. Curse of Debilitation: the effects are vanilla's (Poison does not take on undead, Wither does not take on wither skeletons); Wither II until death kills a mob slowly, as asked. The Damage upgrade's description now says it does not change the curse.
* **Validation.** Unit tests 345 pass. Real client (`tools/qa/QaFire.java.txt`, 61 checks): standalone 61 of 61, pack 61 of 61, with a real TaCZ bullet (+30 percent on both of its damage parts). What each run found: `docs/ROUND-0036-TESTING.md`.
* **Not tested.** A real raid with real raiders walking through the three (a harness raid state with raiders without AI was used); the carpet's fire blocks under a raider with AI; a dedicated server; the owner's pack with the real gunpacks (only TaCZ's default AK-47 was fired); a flare thrown by hand through a placed cannon; saving and reloading marked mobs; a witch drinking Fire Resistance while Ablaze, an enderman hit by Spotter Arrows, a raid boss set Ablaze. Sounds were not listened to.
* **Next steps.** Owner playtest of the three in a real raid (pack edition, real gunpacks); decide on the napalm fire blocks (decision 5); balance Ablaze's 4 a second and the 30 percent.

## Stuck raiders (0.0.13)

* **Superseded in 0.0.14:** the silent rescue described here became a visible gate (see above); the tracker, the busy rules, the ladder (now `MAX_GATES`, `arsenalGates`), the destination search and the diagnostics are the same code, and the spawn-corridor filtering is unchanged.
* **Problem.** A wave ends only when `spawnRemaining==0 && d.raiders.isEmpty()` and `raidTicks>24000` fails the raid, so one raider that cannot arrive could lose it. Owner constraints: raiders keep marching straight (no detour pathfinding, `BeaconCombat.approach` movement unchanged), flyers/gliders, parachutists in the air, Special Forces soldiers and siege-creeper ignition are out of scope, no new packet.
* **Code map.** `RaidMarch` (pure decisions and the transient state keyed by UUID: `Track`, `observe`, `stuck`, `busy`, `next`, `afterNoDestination`; constants next to `DIG_RANGE`), `RaidRescue` (the world-touching part, called once a second per raider from the raid loop in `ArsenalBeacon.raid`; relocation; withdrawal; the `[stuck]` log), `RaidSpawns` (12 wedges, `corridor`/`score`/`probe`, the new `find`, `findRescue`, `rescueOrder`, `pickRescue`, `farFromPlayers`), hooks in `RaidBreaching.breach` (records a breach) and `BeaconCombat.approach` (records that the forced-move fallback ran). The rules are in the GDD (section 5.2 and the designer guide); this section only records decisions and what is untested.
* **Decisions that go beyond the written spec.** (1) Busy time restarts the 25 s clock instead of merely exempting the moment: a ranged raider that lost line of sight after a long shoot-out gets 25 more seconds. (2) Extra exemptions: a player within 12 blocks while something hurt the raider in the last 5 s (a bystander alone does not count), and a chunk that is not entity-ticking. (3) A stuck raider for which no rescue spot exists is retried after the 10 s grace window and, if ordinary, withdrawn after three dry attempts; bosses keep retrying. (4) The corridor cache is per world-aligned 30 degree wedge (the offset used to be random per call, so a sector index meant nothing): the probe samples the wedge's centre ray every 4 blocks from 8 blocks beyond the zone to the farthest ring and scores any spawn distance from the same array, so there are at most 12 probes per raid. A candidate's real line can differ by up to 15 degrees: it is a heuristic. (5) Tree trunks and player-placed blocks on the ray count as unknown, not as steps.
* **Behaviour to expect.** Players who trap raiders in a pit see them moved out after 25 s and withdrawn after the fourth stall. A swarm raid now spawns from the clean wedges only while any exist, so it can come from fewer directions than before.
* **Opt-in switches.** `-Darsenal.stuckLog=true` (diagnostics: one `[stuck] event=STUCK|RESCUE|NO_PLACE|WITHDRAW ...` line each, see `docs/validation/stuck-raiders/gametests-and-diagnostics.txt` for real lines), `-Darsenal.stuckTests=true` (registers `StuckRaiderGameTests` and `/stuck-raider-test`). ## Mess Hall v4 decisions and status

* **Sunlight only:** raider daylight ignition is blocked at `Mob.isSunBurnTick`; the blanket `clearFire` tick is removed. Flame arrows and fire traps work subject to native species immunity and trap adaptation. Production mapping uses MixinGradle's generated refmap.
* **Trap adaptation:** current-raid attackers learn eleven tagged damage classes only after positive damage. The first damaging wave stays at normal damage; each later wave gains 25% resistance, reaching immunity after four steps. Each class announces its steps and is saved in `CampaignData.TrapFirstWave`; new raids/reset clear it. Owned/attributed weapons and support cannon damage are excluded. Ownerless custom turrets need tags/markers if they discard attribution. See `docs/RAID-ADAPTATION.md`.
* **Backend:** require leopoko **TaCZ Attributes 1.4** (`tacz_attributes`, exact `[1.4]`, CurseForge project 1113285/file 8470731), clients and server in both editions. Global `gun_damage` and `reload_speed` receive stable meal-owned transient `MULTIPLY_TOTAL` modifiers of amount `b`, preserving other values and yielding `1+b`. Former Firepower hit listener and `MealReloadMixin`/`ReloadClock` accelerator are removed. TaCZ compile/runtime pin is **1.1.8-hotfix2**, project 1028108/file **9037989**. No dependency JAR is embedded.
* **Reload:** one backend timing path; rate changes rebase accumulated progress, and native staged timestamp deltas survive backend restoration via a cached, type-checked field bridge and one native call. Reload clips are selected by animation name across track metadata, including transitions; neutral/finished/swapped runners reset to 1. Custom private script timers/animation conventions remain a compatibility limit.
* **Combat adapters:** Brawler/Heavy Hand retain one native bash hook without adding Strength/Might again. Demolition uses actual explosive kinetic projectile behavior/owner, not an `rpg` category. Optional LesRaisins `lrtactical` 0.4.3 (`[0.4.3,0.4.4)`) scales its shared grenade detonation radius once. Absent/other optional versions stay inactive. Power/fuse/destruction settings remain native. TAA, GunsmithLib, Apotheosis, Apothic Attributes and TaCZ:Accel are not mandatory.
* **Preserved:** recipes, costs, servings, pot capacities, traits/pairs, models, vanilla display, baseline values and existing meal saves/lifecycle. Protocol stays **24**, with no packet changes. No legacy footprint migration is needed under the owner's fresh-world policy.
* **Delivery/verification:** local test bundle with both editions and separate TaCZ Attributes 1.4 JARs. Both editions build; 70 beacon tests pass (7 guide results remain Gradle up-to-date); 20 standalone server cases, optional LesRaisins ownership/radius, a real stop/restart fixture, and native real-client reload speeds pass. Runtime testing fixed the cross-mixin timestamp bridge and dripstone classification. See `docs/MESS-HALL-V4-TESTING.md` and its validation JSON for client status, exact evidence and remaining coverage.

## Historical Mess Hall v3 decisions and status

* **Models:** the artist package (`Create-Arsenal-Mess-Hall.zip`) is installed: halls are **2 wide x 1 deep x 2 tall** (anchor, right cell, and both above), the pot **1 x 1 x 2**; front is the serving side. `tools/kitchen/generate_resources.py` no longer rewrites the model geometry (blockstates, item models, recipes, tags and traits are still generated). The menu board text of the pot is drawn by `KitchenRenderer` on the chalkboard of the upper block.
* **Meal display:** the custom HUD and its packet are removed. A meal is shown with vanilla potion effects, one per bonus (`meal_<id>`, amplifier = strength - 1 + 4 for a pair), endless at home plus a Home Zone effect, finite and counting down in the field. The server (`PlayerMeals.syncEffects`) keeps them in step; the meal is server state, so removing the display only hides it until the next sync. Forge asks for the client extension from inside the `MobEffect` constructor: never read subclass fields there (a real crash found by the real-client QA).
* **Effects:** 16 (nine from v2 plus Might, Agility, Fortune, Recovery, Hearth, Springy Step, Strong Swimmer); every vanilla food has a trait; a stew doubles an effect when two different foods of the same `family` feed it. **Gun effect values were raised** (Firepower 8%, Quick Hands 10%, Brawler 20%, Heavy Hand 15%, Demolition 10%): the v2 values passed the TaCZ server tests but could not be felt. If the owner still feels nothing, the remaining cause is that TaCZ's reload and bash animations keep their own speed (only the server state finishes earlier); remove the five effects if they cannot be made visible.
* **Raids:** raiders march straight at the beacon and dig only when `RaidMarch.idle` (under 1 block in a second). The old breach check ran only when `gameTime%40==0` inside a loop that runs when `clock%20==0`; the two counters have an arbitrary offset, so in most worlds it practically never fired (this also kept siege creepers from digging). Raiders carry a red exclamation mark and are sun-proof; spawns are open surface ground at or above the beacon (`RaidSpawns.openGround`, relaxed after 30 s / 90 s); vexes and phantoms are steered straight in by `RaidTypes.glide` (their own AI is off: a mob without AI is not moved by vanilla, so they are moved by hand); the marks packet is idempotent and resent every second (fixes paratroopers without a parachute: the one-shot packet could arrive before the client knew the mob).
* **Sign post:** the post of the platform sign is drawn without culling (a flat strip vanished when seen from its back).

## Historical Mess Hall v2 decisions and status

* Stew costs **4/7/10/12 ingredient items** for the existing **4/8/12/16 servings**, tuned in `MealRules.tier`. Reserve one of each composition type, then distribute extra cost across stacks in slot order, round by round. Mk I supports 2–4 distinct types; later tiers 2–6. Sandwich uses 2–3 types, normally one staple and one or two fillings, one item each. All failures remain atomic; every consumed container ingredient returns its remainder.
* Composition scores each distinct item type once, independent of its quantity or duplicate slots. Datapack overlaps merge by maximum; types then sum, strongest effects win, strength remains capped at 3. Added tags/definitions for all five new IDs: beef `firepower`, honey `quick_hands`, pork `brawler`, golden carrot `heavy_hand`, pufferfish `demolition`. Existing saved meals retain their compositions.
* Base FIELD bonuses: Firepower **+5% gun damage**, Quick Hands **+6% reload speed**, Brawler **+15% gun bash damage**, Heavy Hand **+12% gun bash knockback**, Demolition **+5% launcher explosion radius**. Same 1/1.25/1.5 strength scaling, 2× HOME, frozen/reduced field time, replacement, UUID persistence and death clearing as existing meals.
* TaCZ 1.1.8-hotfix2 integration: native `EntityHurtByGunEvent.Pre` for damage; native reload timestamp advancement in `LivingEntityReload.tickReloadState`; only `ModernKineticGunItem.doPerLivingHurt` bash arguments; only `ExplodeUtil.createExplosion` radius for player-owned native kinetic projectiles whose gun index is `rpg` (M320, RPG-7 and compatible launchers). Three narrowly targeted `@Pseudo` mixins and reflection follow existing project conventions. Both editions already require TaCZ; no dependency policy changed.
* Protocol **23-pack / 23-standalone**: cost/availability/per-slot deductions in server preview; all five effect IDs and base amounts in pot board/chat; FIELD/HOME amounts in preview tooltips and actual active amounts in the HUD. The HUD now lists all effects. Both editions share gameplay.
* Unsupported: separate thrown-grenade entities, non-launcher explosive ammunition, custom scripts bypassing TaCZ's native reload clock/bash/explosion paths. Vanilla melee, TNT/creepers and unrelated mod explosives get no meal bonus. Animation clips keep their own playback speed while native server reload state/ammunition complete sooner. See `docs/MESS-HALL.md` for exact integration points.
