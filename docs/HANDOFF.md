# Handoff: where the project stands

Last updated for **1.1.1** on branch `feature/loadout-station` (2026-10-14): the owner's list after trying 1.1.0 (station clicks, the wardrobe column, the plunging capsule, the Loadout radio lines, Exchange, Support shop, attachment types, MRE, Mess Hall, cook pot). 1.1.0 (the Loadout Station) is the section after it. `dev` stays at **1.0.1** and **`main` at 1.0.0** (tag `v1.0.0`) until the owner asks for a merge. ## 1.1.1: the owner's list after 1.1.0 (branch `feature/loadout-station`)

* **What it is.** The owner, after trying 1.1.0 (with a screenshot of the coin slots' numbers overlapping their counts): "Rightclick open the UI, sneak click quick print. reverse the current setup"; the number row redesigned, "the loadout coin slot should be vertical while the insert bar for the coin making should be horizontal", "Current active loadout should have a text next to it"; for the Loadout Flare "Don't use the parachute, i want the capsule to flies down at incredible speed, impacting with a satisfying sound like an explosion, then automatically opens"; their four Loadout radio lines (`Loadout_chatters.rar`, the no-voice build keeps its sound). Side quest: "a short vanilla eating sound when you eat stew at the cook pot with a bowl". Then a second list: Exchange scroll bar, Support shop hover text, attachment table sorting by type like the armor table, the Mess Hall sandwich hint clipping, and MRE food slots that take only different foods.
* **Station clicks** (`LoadoutStation.use`, `LoadoutPrint.Events.quickPrint`, `LoadoutCinematic`). Any right-click opens the screen; `isSecondaryUseActive()` prints the active slot. Vanilla skips a block's `use` when a sneaking player holds an item, so a `RightClickBlock` handler sets `useBlock` to ALLOW on station cells (`LoadoutStation.cell`). The camera's sneak skip needs a fresh press: `sneakFreeFor` remembers the print during which sneak was seen up. `LoadoutStation.door` is gone (nothing used it any more).
* **The wardrobe** (`LoadoutMenu`, `LoadoutScreen`). `W=408`; coin slots in a column at `COIN_X=165`, `COIN_Y=44`, a row every 20 px (`COIN_ROW`); the pack area and the player's inventory moved right (`INV_X=238`); the four buttons in a 2 x 2 grid under the fee (which wraps to two lines). Coin slots are active on the Locker page too, and a coin shift-clicks into them there. A click anywhere on a row sends the SELECT button (then the slot click goes on as usual). New lang keys `loadout.shelf_tip`, `loadout.active_tag`; `loadout.shelf` is "Wardrobe".
* **The capsule** (`LoadoutCapsule.Drop`, `impact`, `CapsuleEntity`; `LoadoutClient.dropTick/dropLanded`; `OrdnanceClient.shake`). `deliver` picks the cell first and spawns the drop `CAPSULE_DROP_HEIGHT` (96) above it with its target y in synced entity data; the drop has `noPhysics` and moves `CAPSULE_FALL_SPEED` (4) a tick, clamped at the target, on both sides; the flare spawns it `CAPSULE_FALL_TICKS` (24) before the countdown ends (`LoadoutRules.capsuleFallStart`), outdoors and under cover alike (it passes through roofs; Quantum Tunneling and the Dimensional Link still decide whether a call is taken). The landing: `impact` (explosion and anvil sounds, explosion emitter, explosions, floor block particles, smoke, a cloud ring; nothing broken, nobody hurt), then `place`, then the door opens by itself at `autoOpenAt` (`CAPSULE_OPEN_DELAY` 8 ticks). Client: a smoke and flame trail, the model drawn full bright and turning, and one view shake per drop (3.2 degrees, 40 blocks, 26 ticks) through the shared `OrdnanceClient.shake`. `closed()` now leaves a capsule with items open; an emptied one shuts and folds. `OpenAt`, `CloseAt` and `AutoOpenAt` are saved. A drop saved by an older build (no target) finds its target from the heightmap.
* **Loadout radio lines.** `import_chatter.py` group "Loadout chatter" (4 lines, 4.64 to 7.6 s, about -18 LUFS, mono); `SupportChatter.LOADOUT_LINES/LOADOUT_TICKS` (119, 100, 93, 152), `LONGEST` 160; `SupportCalls.Kind.voice()` removed (every kind has its own lines); events `flare.loadout_chatter_1..4`, subtitle "Loadout radio answers"; `voice-replacements.json` gives them the supply call's `block.note_block.bit` at pitch 1.2.
* **Other screens.** `ExchangeScreen`: a 6 px scroll bar (drag the thumb or click the track; the wheel as before), "Offers 1 to 7 of 16" under the list (it was drawn behind the tabs), full offer names on hover when cut. `SupportScreen.productTip`: the product's name, its note in full, then the item's own tooltip lines. `AttachmentTypes` (new): TaCZ's `IAttachment.getType` mapped to sight, muzzle, stock, grip, laser, extended_mag, other; `WeaponPlatform` counts and filters attachment tables by it, `PlatformScreen` gives them the armor table's type button and picker (`typedGear()`). `MessHallScreen`: the sandwich hint 3 px higher. `CookPot`: `GENERIC_EAT` when served. `MreStation`: `taken()` keeps a food out of a second slot; `check()` returns `same_food` for a repeat (`MreRules.allDifferent`).
* **Validation.** 444 unit tests pass. In game (`QaLoadout`, with Create): **134 of 134 checks pass** in the final run (run 15); screenshots and check lines in `docs/validation/release-1.1.1/`, the full list in `docs/ROUND-1.1.1-TESTING.md`. Fixed on the way: the impact shake missed when the client's capsule was removed too high; the player sliding up to a block past the exit cell after a print (now no speed on the last held step, and a snap to the exit cell when more than `LoadoutRules.RELEASE` = 0.5 blocks off as the doors close); the Loadout Station's Workshop Fabrication card had no name or description (owner's report; a test now checks every card). ## 1.1.0: the Loadout Station (branch `feature/loadout-station`)

* **What it is.** The owner's request: "New feature new git branch, 1.1.0 Loadout station", with an art pack (`Create-Arsenal-Loadout.zip`) and a full specification: a 3 x 3 x 3 Loadout Station that prints a saved kit onto a player with a short camera sequence, Loadout Coins that hold the kit, and a Loadout Flare that brings the kit in a parachuted capsule. Built on its own branch from `dev` at 1.0.1; `dev` and `main` are unchanged.
* **Art.** The pack is kept in `docs/art/Eternal-Defense-Loadout` (renamed from the zip's folder so no old project name is left; the files and the README's line numbers are as delivered). `tools/ui-assets/import_loadout.py` (re-runnable) installs `minecraft-models/block` and `item` and the two textures into `assets/eternal_defense`, rewrites the models' namespace, checks the README's face counts and writes the blockstates of the station, the capsule and their invisible cells. The viewer page in the pack is ignored. Every number of the README lives in one class, `LoadoutModel`, each with a comment naming its README line; `LoadoutModelTest` parses the installed JSON (cubes inside -16..32, points inside the bounds, the door's travel fits, the face counts) and nothing else hard-codes those numbers. **PLACEHOLDER:** the Loadout Coin and Loadout Flare item icons are drawn by `tools/ui-assets/make_sprites.py` (`sprites.py`: `loadout_coin`, `flare('loadout')`) until the owner sends art.
* **Station** (`LoadoutStation`, `LoadoutData`). Anchor in the middle (draws the model, no shape), 26 invisible parts with the model's collision (`LoadoutModel.cellBoxes`); the player being printed collides with none of it. Placement: bottom-centre on the clicked spot, all 27 cells free, Home Zone only, facing the player. Unbreakable in survival while a coin slot holds coins ("Empty the machine first"); blast resistance 3,600,000; fireproof item; creative can break it (coins and Locker drop). SavedData `eternal_defense_loadout` by machine id (coins, remembered kinds, Locker; players' active slots and cooldowns by UUID). BER (`LoadoutClient.StationRenderer`) draws the doors, the ring and both arms from the README's 80-tick cycle; render box inflated by 2 blocks; sparks at the 4 nozzles follow the ring, steam at the 4 vents at tick 64.
* **Coin** (`LoadoutCoin`): 14 stacks saved with every tag; refused with a coin, a filled container (any item with a non-empty item handler, shulker boxes included) or over `kitLimitKilobytes` (64). Fee 4 Reinforced Beacon Plating + 1 Ardent Energy through the supply source (pack, then Hubs with the Hub chat line).
* **Menu** (`LoadoutMenu`, `LoadoutScreen`): one container screen, 384 x 240; vanilla menu buttons and container data only. Pack area, fee line, Make coin, 9 shared coin slots (each remembers its kind; Forget clears an empty slot's kind), Set active, Print, Locker page; "In use by NAME" and Print off while busy.
* **Closet** (owner, mid-round: "a preview of the player model after equiping the kit ... like browsing a clothes closet ... Don't be afraid to enlarge the UI"). `KitMannequin` (client): a `RemotePlayer` with the local player's profile, dressed in a kit (feet, legs, chest, head, off hand, first hotbar item in the main hand), drawn with `InventoryScreen.renderEntityInInventory` inside a scissored backdrop with a rail, lamp, light cone and a brass stand; it turns slowly by itself and follows a mouse drag; its name tag is suppressed (`RenderNameTagEvent` DENY while it is drawn). The station's closet shows the kit being packed when the pack area holds anything, else the selected coin slot's kit (< and > step through the 9 slots; caption "Slot N", "Slot N · active" on the active one, and a summary "N armor, off hand, N items"). The capsule screen (332 x 200) shows the kit inside on the same figure.
* **Print** (`LoadoutPrint`, `LoadoutRules.Timeline`): refusals before anything changes; entry walk at 0.2 blocks/tick (at least 12 ticks) with the doors opening; the README's 80-tick cycle; tick 40 the single transaction; exit walk to the cell 2 blocks in front; doors close in 12 ticks; then the 30 s cooldown. Held players: no input, interaction, attack, item use, drop (given back) or damage (damage that bypasses invulnerability still goes through, so /kill and the void work); position corrected by the server beyond 0.75 blocks from where the path was up to 3 ticks ago (`LoadoutRules.drift`, `LAG_TICKS`), and the client catches up to the path at no more than 0.45 blocks a tick (`CATCH_UP`) when it learns of a print late, so neither side jumps. No seat entity. State in the anchor's update tag for late joiners.
* **Camera** (`LoadoutCinematic`, `mixin/LoadoutCameraMixin`, `mixin/LoadoutHandMixin`): ENTRY, PRINT (from the cavity's top front corner looking down, wide FOV, flash, steam, sound at tick 40), EXIT pull-back into first person; HUD and hand hidden (TaCZ draws its first-person gun outside `RenderHandEvent`, so `LoadoutHandMixin` skips `GameRenderer.renderItemInHand` while a shot is on), nothing in the options changed; sneak or Esc skips; any fault, logout, death, dimension change or broken station ends it; `loadoutCinematic` client config.
* **Flare and capsule** (`LoadoutCall`, `LoadoutCapsule`, `SupportFlares`): Loadout Flare 5 Ardent at the Support Platform; Supply Flare timing; underground needs Quantum Tunneling; other dimensions need the Dimensional Link (its Field Guide text updated); needs a station, the cannon and a coin in the active slot (else a Hub); the coin is taken only when the cannon fires; one call at a time; capsule 1 x 1 x 2 with the parachute at the chute anchor, faces its owner, nearest two-high cell within 3 blocks else the old parcel; owner only; door slides in 10 ticks (BER); Equip fills empty slots only; empty and closed it folds away with steam; unopened it lasts like a parcel.
* **Network.** No packet added or changed: protocol stays 41 (menu buttons use the existing vanilla button packet; the print and the capsule travel in block update tags).
* **Decisions and interpretations to confirm with the owner.**
  1. **Price (flagged):** 64 Reinforced Beacon Plating, 8 Restoration Matrices, 32 iron, 16 glass, the flagship of Workshop Fabrication (`Economy.LOADOUT_*`).
  2. The sounds are vanilla placeholders: `doorSound` piston extend, `steamSound` fire extinguish (`[loadout]` in `eternal-defense-common.toml`).
  3. The Loadout Station does not add to the base score's installations (it is not one of the 20 counted since 0.0.41).
  4. A capsule's "nearest two-high cell within 3 blocks" may be above or below the flare's cell (on a low roof, for instance).
  5. The 9 coin slots are shared by everyone at the base; the active slot and the cooldown are per player.
  6. A coin in the main hand prints when the active slot is empty and of the slot's kind (or any coin if the slot has no kind).
* **Validation.** 434 unit tests pass (`LoadoutModelTest` 11, `LoadoutRulesTest` 16 new). Real client (`tools/qa/QaLoadout.java.txt`, xvfb, TaCZ and TaCZ Attributes, no Create): **104 of 104 checks pass** in the final run, screenshots and check lines in `docs/validation/release-1.1.0/`, the full list in `docs/ROUND-1.1.0-TESTING.md`. Measured: a full coin (kit A, 14 stacks, a TaCZ AK with 4 attachments) is **1,560 bytes** of NBT; the print takes **127 ticks (6.35 s)** from 2.5 blocks out; the walk was 0.000 blocks off the synced path from tick 10 with 0 server corrections when seen through the player's own camera (3 corrections when the test watched through a still camera, because a client whose camera is another entity sends no positions); the glow parts kept 96 to 100 % of their day brightness at night while unlit parts fell to 57 %.
* **Not tested.** Two real clients (the race and the busy lock used a server-side fake player, the late joiner a block entity rebuilt from its update tag); a real logout and relog during a print or the cooldown; a dedicated server, real network latency, the full 326-mod pack, Create in the same client; sounds (no audio device); a capsule left 30 minutes, with its owner offline or landing in water; backpacks and bundles in the pack area; real right-click placement facing east, south or west and on uneven ground; dragging the closet figure; the screens at window sizes other than 1280 x 720.
* **Next steps.** Owner: try 1.1.0 (the jars in the test zip), say whether the price, the sounds and the camera feel right, and send art for the coin and flare icons. Merge `feature/loadout-station` into `dev` when the owner says so.
