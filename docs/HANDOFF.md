## Raid music player (0.0.17)

* **What it is.** Owner request: a little music player that shows the song's name below the beacon status card, always visible while a raid runs (unlike the card, which needs the beacon nearby or the shovel); beacon Settings switches to turn the music and the player off; the owner's songs (sent as MP3 in a RAR: Normal/4, Boss/1, Special raid/1), the ordinary ones randomised, each wave starting a song and looping it until the next wave changes to another.
* **Code map.** `RaidPlaylist` (pure: the song table, pools, `cue`, `seed`, `pick`, `clock`), `RaidMusic` (client: playback with its own `Track` sound instances on the Music channel, fades, the fanfare, the hold on the game's music, `drawPlayer`), `mixin.RaidMusicMixin` (now on `MusicManager.tick`: the game's music manager waits while `RaidMusic.holdsMusic()`), `ArsenalClientConfig` (client config: `raidMusic`, `musicPlayer`), `BeaconClient.hud`/`card`/`raidSong` (the card's position is computed even when it is hidden, and the player is drawn under it), the Settings tab in `BeaconClient.ControlScreen` (two columns), `ArsenalSounds.song(id)` (one `music.<id>` event per song).
* **Songs.** `tools/audio/import_songs.py <folder> <out>` converts the owner's MP3s (silence trimmed at both ends, two-pass linear loudness normalisation to -16 LUFS, cover art and tags dropped, Vorbis q4 48 kHz). The 0.0.16 themes are tuned to the same loudness through `volume` in `sounds.json`. Adding a song: put the file in `sounds/music/`, add a `music.<id>` entry to `sounds.json` (with `"stream": true`), a `RaidPlaylist.SONGS` row (id, pool, length in seconds; `MusicPlayerTest` checks the length against the file) and a `gui.arsenal_beacon.music.<id>` name.
* **Decisions.** (1) The 0.0.16 themes stay in the pools, so the boss and special pools also have two songs to alternate between waves (drop a row in `RaidPlaylist.SONGS` to retire one). (2) The order is random but seeded by the beacon position and raid number, so every player hears the same song and a reconnect does not reshuffle. (3) The boss pool plays on the boss wave (the last wave) of a hard raid only; 0.0.16 played the boss theme for the whole hard raid. (4) The music plays for every player in the Overworld during a raid, not only near the beacon (the request said the player always shows during a raid). (5) Playback is done by the mod (not the music manager) so it can loop seamlessly and fade; the game's music waits 30 s after the raid music ends. (6) Raid music off also turns the fanfare off. (7) The card moves up by half the player's height during a raid so the pair stays centred.
* **Credits.** The six songs are by leonpentastorm, made with Suno (from the MP3 tags), supplied by the owner; treat them like the display artwork (All Rights Reserved by their creator). The bundle's THIRD-PARTY.md says so.
* **QA harness.** `tools/qa/QaWorld.java.txt` scenario `r7` (flag file `/tmp/claude-0/qa-r7.flag`; parts `music settings menu pictures loop fire`): `echo all > /tmp/claude-0/qa-r7.flag; tools/qa/setup-qa.sh <dir> standalone QaWorld`, then `runClient` under `xvfb-run`. To record the game's audio on a machine without a sound card, start the client with `ALSOFT_CONF` pointing at an OpenAL Soft config with `drivers = wave` and `[wave] file = <path>.wav` (the log then says "OpenAL initialized on device Wave File Writer"); cross-correlate 2-second windows of the capture against the files in `sounds/music/` to see which song played when. Run the victory step on the standalone edition only (the pack's completion cache needs Create, absent in the development runtime).
* **Validation.** `docs/MUSIC-PLAYER-TESTING.md`, evidence in `docs/validation/music-player/`: 158 unit tests; standalone real client 46/48 (the 2 fails were the harness waiting for a one-tick phase, fixed; the recording shows the fanfare playing at the victory); pack real client 24/24 including a 75-second loop of a 64-second song; the recorded audio shows the right song for every wave, the fanfare at VICTORY, silence after, and a seamless loop seam; the six new fire supports, the scrolling cannon menu, the maxed-upgrade tooltip, the falling shell and bomb, and the 11 gate GameTests with the exit gate (these close the 0.0.16 gap).
* **Bundle of the merged `dev`.** `python3 tools/release/package-dev.py --backend-jar <TaCZ Attributes 1.4 jar>` (after `build releaseJars`) writes `dist/Arsenal-Dev-0.0.17-pack-test.zip` and `dist/Arsenal-Dev-0.0.17-standalone-test.zip` (one per edition, each under the 30 MB chat upload limit) and their `.sha512` files.

## Stuck raiders (0.0.13)

* **Superseded in 0.0.14:** the silent rescue described here became a visible gate (see above); the tracker, the busy rules, the ladder (now `MAX_GATES`, `arsenalGates`), the destination search and the diagnostics are the same code, and the spawn-corridor filtering is unchanged.
* **Problem.** A wave ends only when `spawnRemaining==0 && d.raiders.isEmpty()` and `raidTicks>24000` fails the raid, so one raider that cannot arrive could lose it. Owner constraints: raiders keep marching straight (no detour pathfinding, `BeaconCombat.approach` movement unchanged), flyers/gliders, parachutists in the air, Special Forces soldiers and siege-creeper ignition are out of scope, no new packet.
* **Code map.** `RaidMarch` (pure decisions and the transient state keyed by UUID: `Track`, `observe`, `stuck`, `busy`, `next`, `afterNoDestination`; constants next to `DIG_RANGE`), `RaidRescue` (the world-touching part, called once a second per raider from the raid loop in `ArsenalBeacon.raid`; relocation; withdrawal; the `[stuck]` log), `RaidSpawns` (12 wedges, `corridor`/`score`/`probe`, the new `find`, `findRescue`, `rescueOrder`, `pickRescue`, `farFromPlayers`), hooks in `RaidBreaching.breach` (records a breach) and `BeaconCombat.approach` (records that the forced-move fallback ran). The rules are in the GDD (section 5.2 and the designer guide); this section only records decisions and what is untested.
* **Decisions that go beyond the written spec.** (1) Busy time restarts the 25 s clock instead of merely exempting the moment: a ranged raider that lost line of sight after a long shoot-out gets 25 more seconds. (2) Extra exemptions: a player within 12 blocks while something hurt the raider in the last 5 s (a bystander alone does not count), and a chunk that is not entity-ticking. (3) A stuck raider for which no rescue spot exists is retried after the 10 s grace window and, if ordinary, withdrawn after three dry attempts; bosses keep retrying. (4) The corridor cache is per world-aligned 30 degree wedge (the offset used to be random per call, so a sector index meant nothing): the probe samples the wedge's centre ray every 4 blocks from 8 blocks beyond the zone to the farthest ring and scores any spawn distance from the same array, so there are at most 12 probes per raid. A candidate's real line can differ by up to 15 degrees: it is a heuristic. (5) Tree trunks and player-placed blocks on the ray count as unknown, not as steps.
* **Behaviour to expect.** Players who trap raiders in a pit see them moved out after 25 s and withdrawn after the fourth stall. A swarm raid now spawns from the clean wedges only while any exist, so it can come from fewer directions than before.
* **Opt-in switches.** `-Darsenal.stuckLog=true` (diagnostics: one `[stuck] event=STUCK|RESCUE|NO_PLACE|WITHDRAW ...` line each, see `docs/validation/stuck-raiders/gametests-and-diagnostics.txt` for real lines), `-Darsenal.stuckTests=true` (registers `StuckRaiderGameTests` and `/stuck-raider-test`). ## Mess Hall v4 decisions and status

* **Sunlight only:** raider daylight ignition is blocked at `Mob.isSunBurnTick`; the blanket `clearFire` tick is removed. Flame arrows and fire traps work subject to native species immunity and trap adaptation. Production mapping uses MixinGradle's generated refmap.
* **Trap adaptation:** current-raid attackers learn eleven tagged damage classes only after positive damage. The first damaging wave stays at normal damage; each later wave gains 25% resistance, reaching immunity after four steps. Each class announces its steps and is saved in `CampaignData.TrapFirstWave`; new raids/reset clear it. Owned/attributed weapons and support cannon damage are excluded. Ownerless custom turrets need tags/markers if they discard attribution. See `docs/RAID-ADAPTATION.md`.
* **Backend:** require leopoko **TaCZ Attributes 1.4** (`tacz_attributes`, exact `[1.4]`, CurseForge project 1113285/file 8470731), clients and server in both editions. Global `gun_damage` and `reload_speed` receive stable meal-owned transient `MULTIPLY_TOTAL` modifiers of amount `b`, preserving other values and yielding `1+b`. Former Firepower hit listener and `MealReloadMixin`/`ReloadClock` accelerator are removed. TaCZ compile/runtime pin is **1.1.8-hotfix2**, project 1028108/file **9037989**. No dependency JAR is embedded.
* **Reload:** one backend timing path; rate changes rebase accumulated progress, and native staged timestamp deltas survive backend restoration via a cached, type-checked field bridge and one native call. Reload clips are selected by animation name across track metadata, including transitions; neutral/finished/swapped runners reset to 1. Custom private script timers/animation conventions remain a compatibility limit.
* **Combat adapters:** Brawler/Heavy Hand retain one native bash hook without adding Strength/Might again. Demolition uses actual explosive kinetic projectile behavior/owner, not an `rpg` category. Optional LesRaisins `lrtactical` 0.4.3 (`[0.4.3,0.4.4)`) scales its shared grenade detonation radius once. Absent/other optional versions stay inactive. Power/fuse/destruction settings remain native. TAA, GunsmithLib, Apotheosis, Apothic Attributes and TaCZ:Accel are not mandatory.
* **Preserved:** recipes, costs, servings, pot capacities, traits/pairs, models, vanilla display, baseline values and existing meal saves/lifecycle. Protocol stays **24**, with no packet changes. No legacy footprint migration is needed under the owner's fresh-world policy.
* **Delivery/verification:** local test bundle with both editions and separate TaCZ Attributes 1.4 JARs.
